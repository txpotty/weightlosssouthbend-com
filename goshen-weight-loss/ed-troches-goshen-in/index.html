    How ED Troches Work in Goshen

    ED troches usually contain sildenafil, tadalafil or both, which are PDE5 inhibitors. They relax the smooth muscle in penile blood vessels so blood flows in more easily when you are aroused, the same mechanism as standard ED tablets. The troche sits between cheek and gum. Over 10 to 20 minutes it dissolves, with some medication absorbed through the cheek lining and the rest swallowed. Arousal is still required.

    Who ED Troches Are For

    Troches may suit men with ED who have trouble swallowing tablets or simply want a different format.     Troche Dosing and Use in Goshen

    Take a troche as needed, usually 30 to 60 minutes before sex. Tuck it into your cheek and leave it there for 10 to 20 minutes while it dissolves, without chewing or sucking hard. Skip eating and drinking until it is gone. No more than one dose a day. Some troches are scored so you can use half.
