    How GHK-Cu Copper Peptide Therapy Works in South Bend, IN

    How GHK-Cu Works in South Bend

    GHK-Cu is glycine, histidine and lysine holding one copper ion. It was first identified in human plasma, where levels are higher in young adults and decline with age. In laboratory and animal work it appears to increase collagen and elastin production, speed wound repair and reduce inflammatory signals, possibly by carrying copper where tissue needs it. Injectable research in people is limited.

    Who GHK-Cu Is Usually For

    Adults who want firmer skin, fewer fine lines, better scalp health or support for slow-healing tissue are the usual candidates. Some add it during GLP-1 treatment to support skin as weight comes off. Wilson disease, copper allergy, active cancer, pregnancy and breastfeeding usually rule it out, and it has no effect on body weight.

    GHK-Cu Dosing and Schedule in South Bend

    Two forms, different routines. Injections are commonly 1 to 2 milligrams subcutaneously, given daily or a few times a week in cycles of roughly one to two months. Topical cream or serum goes on clean skin once or twice a day. Your provider sets the exact amount and cycle length, and you get written storage and mixing instructions with the shipment.

    What to Expect Over Twelve Weeks

    Little happens in the first two weeks. Collagen remodeling is slow, so visible skin changes typically appear somewhere between week four and week twelve, if they appear at all. Scalp changes run on a slower clock. A full cycle comes before any verdict.

    GHK-Cu Side Effects in South Bend

    Stinging is the most common complaint. Some people see a temporary blue-gray mark at the site, along with redness or itching that settles over a few days. Creams may dry out or irritate sensitive skin, and occasional headache or tiredness has been reported. High doses over long periods raise a theoretical copper excess concern, which is why cycles stay defined.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Before prescribing, a provider reviews your skin goals, health history and supplement list. Expect questions on copper disorders, liver health, allergies, cancer history and pregnancy, plus a specific question about zinc or copper pills since those shift copper levels. Labs are not always needed. Photos of your skin can be uploaded.
